# Checkpoint 5 — Complete Passport

This checkpoint replaces the stale report with a property-specific, navigable Passport and adds an unapplied additive migration for its structured content.

## Navigation

Overview, Timeline, Photos, Storm history, Maintenance, Documents, Policy vault, Certificates, Sources, and Next actions.

## Classification and safety

- Estimates use visible, homeowner-entered, or explicitly disclosed assumptions.
- Representative observations are not diagnoses.
- Regional storm records do not prove an event or damage at the property.
- University hail imagery is omitted until institution, license, date, and property relevance are verified.
- Policy extraction and comparison are neutral aids, not coverage or legal interpretation.
- Roofing, engineering, insurance, public-adjusting, and legal questions route to appropriately qualified or licensed professionals.
- Maintenance does not promise premium reductions, claim prevention, or a particular roof-life outcome.

## Secure surfaces

The authenticated report uses tenant-scoped RLS. The homeowner receives the complete Passport only through the approved opaque token after inspection/documentation authorization. Policy files and private documents are not included in the public-token projection.

## Preview verification required

The Checkpoint 5 migration has not been applied. In an isolated preview Supabase project, verify tenant isolation, document versioning, Storage access, secure homeowner projection, mobile navigation, citation links, and empty/data-filled states before preview deployment.
